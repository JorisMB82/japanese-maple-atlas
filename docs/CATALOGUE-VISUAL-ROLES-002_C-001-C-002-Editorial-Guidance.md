# CATALOGUE-VISUAL-ROLES-002 — C-001 Backfill and C-002 Editorial Guidance

**Status:** EDITORIAL GUIDANCE — media selection and rights review remain pending  
**Authority:** DR-STRATEGY-003, ROADMAP-002B and CATALOGUE-002  
**Applies to:** CUL-000011 through CUL-000020  
**Editorial boundary:** Defines useful subjects, supported caption facts and identity qualifications; does not approve rights, media items, C2 or publication.

## 1. Shared identity-confidence and contribution wording

A lawful source-asserted or community-identified image may illustrate a Catalogue Profile when its identification basis is disclosed, the image does not contradict the profile and it is not used as sole evidence for accepted name, taxon, synonymy or a diagnostic conclusion.

Use this qualification for any non-documented item:

> The source or contributor identifies this plant as the named cultivar. The Japanese Maple Atlas has not independently authenticated the clone. Corrections and corroborating photographs are welcome.

Use this contribution invitation, adapted only for the cultivar name and requested visual role:

> Have a clearer photograph or evidence for this identification? Submit the original image, date, general location, plant label or provenance, and any accession or purchase record through the Atlas contribution path.

## 2. C-001 visual backfill roles

### CUL-000011 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Orange Dream’

- **Habit-primary:** an unobstructed whole-plant view of a mature bushy, rounded specimen, showing crown density, branching and scale.
- **Foliage-detail:** a colour-managed close view of attached spring leaves showing the yellow to yellow-orange ground and any orange-red margin, with enough twig context to avoid presenting a detached leaf as authentication.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** the spring flush at whole-branch or whole-plant scale; an autumn orange-yellow view is the preferred alternative.
- **Optional roles:** summer green-to-lime foliage; bright green young bark with branch context.
- **Visual confusion risks:** RC-009 ‘Katsura’, RC-005 ‘Aureum’, ‘Autumn Moon’ and unnamed yellow-leaved Japanese maples. Similar colour is not sufficient to identify the cultivar.
- **Caption facts supported:** bushy rounded habit; yellow spring foliage with an orange to orange-red edge reported by NC State; greener summer foliage; orange-yellow autumn colour; approximate RHS size of 2.5–4 m. Support: `SRC-CUL-000011-01` and `SRC-CUL-000011-03`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** exact saturation and persistence of the orange-red margin; mature size outside the cited framework; origin history; any claim that colour alone authenticates the clone.
- **Identity disclosure:** use the shared qualification for source-asserted or community-identified items.
- **Contribution prompt:** request a mature habit view plus same-plant spring and autumn photographs, with provenance or label context where available.

### CUL-000012 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Koto-no-ito’

- **Habit-primary:** a whole-plant view of a rounded, bushy, semi-dwarf specimen with enough background separation to show graceful branching and scale.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached leaves showing the long, narrow linearilobum lobes; ideally include both early leaves on older wood and later extension growth from the same plant.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** orange-yellow autumn foliage while the narrow-lobed structure remains visible.
- **Optional roles:** spring foliage with any crimson tint; branch-level view showing the fine leaves and their movement or spacing.
- **Visual confusion risks:** ‘Yamato-koto-no-ito’, ‘Koto-ito-komachi’, other linearilobum cultivars, and finely dissected forms such as RC-002 ‘Seiryu’. The near-names are separate cultivars.
- **Caption facts supported:** rounded semi-dwarf habit; five to seven narrow tapering lobes; green summer foliage; orange-yellow autumn colour; RHS range of about 1.5–2.5 m. Support: `SRC-CUL-000012-01` and `SRC-CUL-000012-02`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** the extent and consistency of early-versus-later leaf-width change; informal nickname usage; any assertion that thread-like foliage alone proves identity.
- **Identity disclosure:** use the shared qualification for source-asserted or community-identified items.
- **Contribution prompt:** request same-plant early- and later-season leaf details plus a whole-plant image and any accession or purchase record.

### CUL-000013 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Inaba-shidare’

- **Habit-primary:** a mature whole-plant view showing the vigorous, arching and cascading crown, preferably with an object or setting that conveys scale without obscuring the plant.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached large, deeply divided purple-red leaves with branch context and neutral colour balance.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** crimson to bright-red autumn foliage on the whole plant or a representative branch.
- **Optional roles:** deep-purple spring flush; a trained higher form showing how an upright framework transitions into cascading branches.
- **Visual confusion risks:** RC-004 ‘Crimson Queen’, ‘Red Dragon’, ‘Tamukeyama’, ‘Orangeola’, ‘Garnet’ and generic red dissectum maples.
- **Caption facts supported:** arching, rounded-cascading habit; large deeply divided purple-red leaves; crimson autumn colour; institutional size ranges that vary materially. Support: `SRC-CUL-000013-01`, `SRC-CUL-000013-02` and `SRC-CUL-000013-03`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** comparative vigour and size; distinction from similar red dissectums based on a single image; exact summer colour under different exposure.
- **Identity disclosure:** use the shared qualification for source-asserted or community-identified items.
- **Contribution prompt:** request a mature habit image, leaf close-up and autumn view from the same plant, together with label or provenance evidence.

### CUL-000014 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Beni-kawa’

- **Habit-primary:** a whole small-tree view showing the upright-rounded to spreading crown and visible young branches.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached five- to seven-lobed leaves photographed with a young red stem so foliage and bark context are linked.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** dormant-season young coral-red stems in natural light, with enough branching context to avoid a generic bark close-up.
- **Optional roles:** spring emergence, which sources describe as pinkish-yellow or soft green; yellow to golden autumn foliage.
- **Visual confusion risks:** RC-003 ‘Sango-kaku’, ‘Aoyagi’, other coral-bark selections and red-stemmed seedlings. Bark colour alone is not diagnostic.
- **Caption facts supported:** coral-red young stems; bushy to upright-rounded small-tree habit; green summer leaves; yellow to golden autumn colour; source-specific spring descriptions. Support: `SRC-CUL-000014-01` and `SRC-CUL-000014-02`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** exact spring colour; comparative size against ‘Sango-kaku’; persistence and intensity of stem colour; clone authentication from bark alone.
- **Identity disclosure:** use the shared qualification for source-asserted or community-identified items.
- **Contribution prompt:** prioritise same-plant dormant stems, leafy habit and autumn foliage, with provenance or a labelled accession where possible.

### CUL-000015 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Trompenburg’

- **Habit-primary:** an unobstructed upright whole-plant view showing crown form and mature scale.
- **Foliage-detail:** a close view from the side and face of attached leaves showing the long, narrow, deeply divided lobes and conspicuously recurved or rolled margins.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** spring deep red-purple foliage or autumn orange-red to crimson foliage while leaf shape remains legible.
- **Optional roles:** late-summer reddish-green or bronze transition; branch-level scale view of the narrow lobes.
- **Visual confusion risks:** RC-001 ‘Bloodgood’, the separate cultivar ‘Green Trompenburg’ and other narrow-lobed red Japanese maples.
- **Caption facts supported:** upright habit; seven, sometimes seven-to-nine, long narrow lobes; rolled or recurved margins; red-purple spring, greener late summer and crimson autumn sequence. Support: `SRC-CUL-000015-01` and `SRC-CUL-000015-02`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** universal mature size; primary origin history; identification based on foliage colour without clear recurved-lobe detail.
- **Identity disclosure:** use the shared qualification for source-asserted or community-identified items.
- **Contribution prompt:** request an upright habit image and sharply focused side/face leaf details, plus provenance distinguishing it from ‘Green Trompenburg’.

## 3. C-002 visual roles

### CUL-000016 — *Acer shirasawanum* ‘Autumn Moon’

- **Habit-primary:** a mature bushy, spreading or upright-rounded whole plant showing the broad crown and scale.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached rounded leaves photographed flat enough to show the full-moon outline and twig context. Prefer the cultivar-specific approximately eleven-lobed form; a broader nine-to-thirteen-lobe range is species-level context and must not be used by itself to authenticate ‘Autumn Moon’.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** spring or early-summer yellow to yellow-green foliage with burnt-orange or salmon tones; an orange-red autumn view is the preferred alternate.
- **Optional roles:** summer foliage showing yellow-green or greener development; paired fruits or branch architecture when clearly attached to the same identified plant.
- **Visual confusion risks:** RC-005 *Acer shirasawanum* ‘Aureum’, ‘Moonrise’, yellow-leaved *Acer palmatum* cultivars and commercial plants labelled *Acer japonicum* ‘Autumn Moon’.
- **Caption facts supported:** current accepted placement in *Acer shirasawanum*; rounded many-lobed foliage; yellow/yellow-green and orange-toned seasonal expression; orange-red autumn colour; site-sensitive scorch risk. Support: `SRC-CUL-000016-01`, `SRC-CUL-000016-02` and `SRC-CUL-000016-03`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** exact orange or salmon intensity; universal mature size; commercial *Acer japonicum* labels; any claim that leaf colour alone distinguishes it from ‘Aureum’.
- **Identity disclosure:** use the shared qualification for non-documented items; state the source label exactly when it uses a historical or commercial taxon formulation.
- **Contribution prompt:** request same-plant spring, summer and autumn photographs plus a clear leaf-outline detail and the original label or provenance.

### CUL-000017 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Waterfall’

- **Habit-primary:** a mature broad, cascading mound photographed from a low-to-mid angle that shows branch fall, crown spread and any trained trunk.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached green dissectum leaves with scale and enough focus to show the narrow deep divisions.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** whole-plant or branch-level autumn colour showing the reported yellow, gold, orange and red mixture.
- **Optional roles:** early bright-green flush; side view of cascading branch architecture.
- **Visual confusion risks:** generic green laceleaf Japanese maples, Dissectum Viride Group plants, RC-002 ‘Seiryu’ and other named green dissectums.
- **Caption facts supported:** broad pendulous mound; seven-to-nine deeply dissected lobes reported by Missouri Botanical Garden; green summer foliage; multicoloured autumn display; documented living specimens around 7 ft. Support: `SRC-CUL-000017-01`, `SRC-CUL-000017-02` and `SRC-CUL-000017-03`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** exact mature size; a universal difference in leaf length from all green dissectums; identification from a generic weeping green plant.
- **Identity disclosure:** use the shared qualification for source-asserted or community-identified items.
- **Contribution prompt:** request a full mature mound, leaf close-up and autumn view from one plant, with accession or purchase evidence.

### CUL-000018 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Aoyagi’

- **Habit-primary:** a mature upright to broad-topped tree with enough trunk and young branch visibility to show green-bark character and scale.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached five-lobed green leaves photographed with a pea-green young twig.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** dormant or leaf-sparse young green stems in neutral light; deep-yellow autumn foliage is the preferred alternate.
- **Optional roles:** spring leaves with purple-red flowers; winged fruits with branch context.
- **Visual confusion risks:** the separate cultivar ‘Yamato-aoyagi’, RC-003 ‘Sango-kaku’, ‘Beni-kawa’, ‘Arakawa’ and ordinary green-stemmed seedlings.
- **Caption facts supported:** accepted *Acer palmatum* name; upright broad-topped habit; vivid pea-green young bark; five-lobed green leaves; deep-yellow autumn colour; a documented JCRA accession measured at 28 ft. Support: `SRC-CUL-000018-01` and `SRC-CUL-000018-02`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** universal mature size; bark-colour intensity; identification from green stems alone; any shortening of ‘Yamato-aoyagi’ to ‘Aoyagi’.
- **Identity disclosure:** use the shared qualification and preserve any source label showing the full cultivar name.
- **Contribution prompt:** request whole-tree, leaf-with-twig and dormant-stem photographs from the same plant, with label or accession evidence.

### CUL-000019 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Shin-deshōjō’

- **Habit-primary:** a compact, bushy or upright-rounded whole plant during the spring flush, with scale visible.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached small five- to seven-lobed leaves showing the intense crimson spring colour; a same-plant summer detail is highly useful.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** a documented spring-to-summer sequence showing transition from crimson through reddish green to green.
- **Optional roles:** reddish-orange autumn colour; summer white speckling where clearly present and photographed without exposure artefacts.
- **Visual confusion risks:** the separate cultivars ‘Deshōjō’ and ‘Deshōjō-nishiki’, RC-009 ‘Katsura’, and other spring-red Japanese maples. Orthographic variants must not be treated as different clones.
- **Caption facts supported:** accepted working form ‘Shin-deshōjō’; compact bushy habit; crimson spring flush; greener summer foliage; reddish-orange autumn colour. Support: `SRC-CUL-000019-01` and `SRC-CUL-000019-02`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** consistency of summer white speckling; exact duration of the crimson flush; distinction from near-name cultivars from one photograph; any implication that spelling on a label proves identity.
- **Identity disclosure:** use the shared qualification; transcribe the source label while normalising the profile link to ‘Shin-deshōjō’.
- **Contribution prompt:** request same-plant spring and summer photographs, a whole-plant view, and a legible label or provenance record showing the original spelling.

### CUL-000020 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Red Dragon’

- **Habit-primary:** a mature compact upright-pendulous to cascading mound showing crown density and scale.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached seven-lobed dissectum leaves with deeply toothed sub-lobes and visible petiole, photographed in neutral colour.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** scarlet or cherry-red spring flush; a flaming scarlet or crimson autumn view is the preferred alternate.
- **Optional roles:** burgundy summer foliage showing colour retention; branch-level view connecting leaf detail to the cascading habit.
- **Visual confusion risks:** RC-004 ‘Crimson Queen’, ‘Inaba-shidare’, ‘Tamukeyama’, ‘Orangeola’, ‘Garnet’ and generic red laceleaf Japanese maples.
- **Caption facts supported:** compact cascading or upright-pendulous mound; seven-lobed foliage cut to the base; scarlet/cherry-red spring, burgundy summer and scarlet/crimson autumn sequence; approximate 1.5–2.5 m garden range. Support: `SRC-CUL-000020-01`, `SRC-CUL-000020-02` and `SRC-CUL-000020-03`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** comparative sun tolerance; exact colour persistence; any claim that red dissected foliage independently authenticates the cultivar.
- **Identity disclosure:** use the shared qualification for source-asserted or community-identified items.
- **Contribution prompt:** request a mature mound, sharply focused foliage and spring or autumn diagnostic view from the same plant, with provenance separating it from other red dissectums.

## 4. Media handoff boundary

Media remains responsible for source discovery, lawful reuse basis, creator and rights-holder attribution, preservation, checksums, derivatives, privacy, media IDs, sidecars and C2 recommendations. This document does not approve any candidate and does not convert a source assertion into clonal authentication.
