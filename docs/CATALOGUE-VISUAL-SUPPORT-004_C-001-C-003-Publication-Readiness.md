## 1. Operational boundary

This document consolidates and updates the operational use of the merged C-001–C-003 profiles and visual-role guidance. It does not replace the canonical profile JSONs, approve a photograph, grant rights, create a media identifier, satisfy C2, publish a profile or alter a governed gap.

The public Visual Library displays approved photography only when the owning cultivar is publicly discoverable. Approved galleries attached to non-public profiles remain governed repository objects until a later C3 publication decision.

For a `source-asserted` or `community-identified` item, use wording equivalent to:

> The source or contributor identifies this plant as the named cultivar. The Japanese Maple Atlas has not independently authenticated the clone. Corrections and corroborating photographs are welcome.

For contributions, request the original file, date, general location, visible label or provenance, and any accession or purchase record. Never imply that appearance alone authenticates a clone.

## 2. C-001 — published profiles

### CUL-000011 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Orange Dream’

- **Current state:** published; approved three-role gallery integrated.
- **Habit-primary:** mature bushy, rounded plant with scale and crown density visible.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached spring leaves showing yellow to yellow-orange colour and any orange-red edge, with twig context.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** spring flush; orange-yellow autumn is the preferred alternate.
- **Optional subjects:** summer lime-green foliage; bright green young bark.
- **Confusion risks:** ‘Katsura’, *Acer shirasawanum* ‘Aureum’, ‘Autumn Moon’ and unnamed yellow-leaved maples.
- **Safe caption facts:** medium bushy habit; yellow to yellow-orange spring foliage; greener summer foliage; orange-yellow autumn colour.
- **Keep qualified:** exact orange-red margin, colour saturation, timing and mature dimensions; colour alone is not identification.
- **Contribution prompt:** request a same-plant spring-to-summer sequence or a mature whole-plant view with label/provenance.

### CUL-000012 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Koto-no-ito’

- **Current state:** published with governed visual gap; institutional permission route active.
- **Habit-primary:** rounded semi-dwarf shrub or small tree showing graceful branching.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached long, thread-like linearilobum leaves, preferably with early and later growth in one sequence.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** orange-yellow autumn foliage.
- **Optional subjects:** crimson-tinted spring flush; branch movement or silhouette without using motion as a diagnostic claim.
- **Confusion risks:** ‘Yamato-koto-no-ito’, ‘Koto-ito-komachi’, ‘Red Pygmy’ and generic narrow-lobed maples.
- **Safe caption facts:** long narrow lobes; green summer foliage; rounded, relatively slow-growing habit; orange-yellow autumn colour.
- **Keep qualified:** quantitative early-versus-later leaf-width change, informal nicknames and exact mature size.
- **Contribution prompt:** request one whole plant, one attached-leaf detail and one autumn image tied to the same labelled specimen.

### CUL-000013 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Inaba-shidare’

### CUL-000014 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Beni-kawa’

- **Current state:** published with governed visual gap; one lawful candidate and institutional permission route recorded.
- **Habit-primary:** compact-to-medium upright or rounded small tree with young branches visible.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached five- to seven-lobed leaf shown together with a red young stem.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** dormant coral-red young stems; this is more useful than a generic summer leaf.
- **Optional subjects:** yellow-gold autumn foliage; comparison of current-year and older stems.
- **Confusion risks:** ‘Sango-kaku’, ‘Aoyagi’, other coral-bark selections and colour-enhanced winter photographs.
- **Safe caption facts:** red young stems; green summer foliage; yellow-to-golden autumn colour; generally smaller published framework than some coral-bark comparisons.
- **Keep qualified:** pinkish-yellow versus soft-green spring emergence, exact stem saturation and bark-based authentication.
- **Contribution prompt:** request a whole plant plus dormant young-stem detail tied to a labelled or accessioned specimen.

### CUL-000015 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Trompenburg’

- **Current state:** published with governed visual gap; strong institutional accession and permission route recorded.
- **Habit-primary:** upright mature crown showing scale and branching.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached narrow, deeply divided lobes photographed from face and side so recurved or rolled margins are visible.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** deep red-purple spring foliage or orange-red to crimson autumn colour.
- **Optional subjects:** late-summer reddish-green transition; comparison frame with the separate cultivar ‘Green Trompenburg’ only when both identities are independently supported.
- **Confusion risks:** ‘Bloodgood’, other narrow-lobed red maples and ‘Green Trompenburg’.
- **Safe caption facts:** upright habit; narrow deeply cut lobes; recurved margins; red-purple spring and orange-red/crimson autumn sequence.

- **Contribution prompt:** request one mature whole plant and one side-angle leaf detail tied to a documented label or accession.

## 3. C-002 — non-public profiles

- **Current state:** non-public; approved lawful three-role gallery integrated; editorial and media inputs are ready for downstream C3 review.
- **Habit-primary:** mature bushy, spreading or upright-rounded whole plant.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached rounded leaves photographed flat with twig context; prefer the cultivar-specific approximately eleven-lobed form.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** yellow to yellow-green spring foliage with burnt-orange or salmon tones; orange-red autumn is the preferred alternate.
- **Optional subjects:** summer yellow-green or greener foliage; fruit or branch architecture tied to the same specimen.

- **Safe caption facts:** accepted Atlas treatment under *Acer shirasawanum*; rounded many-lobed leaves; orange-toned spring and orange-red autumn sequence.
- **Keep qualified:** older taxon labels, nine-to-thirteen-lobe species context, exact colour intensity and mature size.
- **Contribution prompt:** request accession or purchase evidence corroborating the current accepted taxon and cultivar label.

- **Habit-primary:** mature landscape tree showing ordinary crown and trunk scale, not a bonsai substitute.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached green palmate leaf with branch context.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** corky rough bark on older trunk or branches, paired with smoother younger growth.

- **Keep qualified:** exact age of corking, descriptive English aliases, bonsai form and bark-only authentication.
- **Contribution prompt:** request a labelled landscape specimen showing both whole habit and mature rough bark; include age or accession information.

## 5. Editorial publication-readiness conclusion

No canonical profile requires a broad editorial rewrite. The current source packages, identity notes, uncertainty statements and diagnostic comparisons are adequate for Catalogue use. Remaining publication dependencies are principally media completion, rights and identity review, approved sidecar integration, batch approval, protected validation and production inspection.
