## Approval boundary

No entry above is selected or approved. Before promotion, the media stream must obtain original bytes, calculate Atlas SHA-256, preserve item-level rights evidence, visually inspect the source, determine identity confidence, complete a provenance record, process and inspect derivatives, create an approved sidecar and issue a G5 decision.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- `found online` is not a licence;
- `institutional` is not automatic publication permission;
- a Commons category is not identity proof;
- a cultivar label is not clonal authentication;
- a filename is not provenance;
- a candidate status is not approval;
- the raster pipeline does not approve rights or identity.
