# UX-002 — External User Testing Script

**Status:** Ready for controlled testing after Sprint 10 usability remediation  
**Scope:** Interface comprehension and task completion only; Sprint 11 remains out of scope

## Participants

Recruit approximately five participants: two home gardeners or collectors, one nursery or landscape professional, one botanical or horticultural researcher, and one digitally capable person without Japanese-maple expertise.

## Moderator opening

“Please use the Atlas as you naturally would. Think aloud, but I will not teach you the interface. There are no wrong answers; we are testing the product, not you.”

## Tasks

1. Explain what the Atlas helps you do.
2. Find an upright cultivar with finely divided foliage.
3. Compare Seiryu and Crimson Queen.
4. Add at least three cultivars to a research set, then choose which two to compare.
5. Find a cultivar with notable winter interest.
6. Explain why two cultivars are connected in the relationship view or graph.
7. Save and reopen an Explorer view in the browser.
8. Copy a shareable Explorer link.
9. Export the research set in a human-readable format.
10. Find the evidence supporting one cultivar statement.
11. On a phone-sized screen, locate Sources and Repository from the primary navigation.

## Capture

Record task completion, time, wrong turns, terms requiring explanation, confidence after each task, perceived trust, and one final usability score such as SUS. Do not treat facilitator assistance as successful completion.

## Decision gate

Summarize repeated failures and vocabulary problems before resuming roadmap work. Sprint 11 should remain paused until the findings are reviewed and any release-blocking usability issues are resolved.
