## 5. Architecture and morphology consistency

Architecture and leaf morphology remain separate in every record:

- RC-006 — rounded-to-upright crown plus broad palmate leaves;
- RC-007 — upright/bushy crown plus irregular marginal variegation;
- RC-008 — dense upright compact architecture plus small crinkled leaves held in tufts;
- RC-009 — compact upright/vase-shaped juvenile architecture plus small spring-coloured leaves;
- RC-010 — dwarf multibranched architecture plus tightly overlapping layered foliage.

### RC-008 / RC-010 close-comparison rule

The pair is retained because it tests a meaningful diagnostic boundary:

- **RC-008:** greater emphasis on crinkled or curled individual leaves, short internodes and bunching/tufting; taller upright lion’s-mane presentation.
- **RC-010:** greater emphasis on planar overlap, layered branch canopies and dwarf whole-plant scale.

This distinction remains a G4 condition and should be tested with authenticated, same-scale visual material. The drafts do not treat the pair as synonyms or duplicates.

## 8. Horticultural consistency

Common editorial rules applied across Wave 1:

1. full sun/partial shade tags require regional heat, wind and moisture qualification;
2. scorch resistance is never stated absolutely;
3. hardiness systems remain source-specific rather than converted mechanically;
4. soil recommendations distinguish moisture retention from waterlogging;
5. early leaf-out and late-frost risk are explicit for RC-008 and RC-009;
6. relative heat tolerance for RC-010 remains qualified by damage/scorch evidence;
7. use recommendations such as bonsai, container or specimen do not become identity evidence.

## 9. Propagation and authentication consistency

All five records state that:

- vegetative propagation supports cultivar continuity;
- propagation method alone does not authenticate a specimen;
- seed-derived offspring do not inherit the cultivar identity automatically;
- nursery, herbarium, accession and photograph labels are asserted identity unless stronger provenance exists;
- morphology and colour can support a descriptive match but not definitive clonal authentication.

RC-010 explicitly separates NC State's self-seeding statement from cultivar continuity.
