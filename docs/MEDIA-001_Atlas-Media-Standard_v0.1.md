# MEDIA-001 — Atlas Media Standard

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Pilot implementation  
**Introduced:** Sprint 5

## Purpose

The Atlas treats media as a governed knowledge object rather than decorative
page content.

A media object records:

- what the asset depicts;
- which cultivar it belongs to;
- its role in the Atlas;
- whether it is evidence, context, or illustration;
- its source, creator, rights, and status;
- the alt text required for accessible publication;
- the editorial review required before release.

## Core distinction

An Atlas illustration is not automatically evidence.

The current pilot identity plates are classified as:

`illustrative-not-evidence`

They are intended to test:

- consistent visual presentation;
- side-by-side cultivar comparison;
- responsive layouts;
- media metadata;
- accessibility;
- future asset replacement.

## Identity plate standard v1

Pilot identity plates use:

- a 720 × 520 SVG canvas;
- neutral background;
- standardized framing;
- cultivar name and reference identifier;
- representative habit silhouette;
- representative foliage palette;
- explicit label that the image is editorial illustration;
- accessible alt text;
- no claim of photographic or diagnostic fidelity.

## Future photographic policy

Real photographs should eventually record:

- photographer or institution;
- date;
- location;
- specimen or accession when known;
- season and plant part;
- licence or permission;
- authenticity status;
- whether the image supports a specific assertion.

## Publication rule

No generated or editorial image may be presented in a way that could
reasonably be mistaken for an authenticated photograph or primary botanical
observation.
