# DR-STRATEGY-003 — Visual-First Catalogue Owner Approval

**Status:** APPROVED AND ACTIVE  
**Decision owner:** Project Owner / Editor-in-Chief  
**Decision date:** 2026-07-29  
**Applies to:** Catalogue Profiles  
**Does not amend:** Reference Standard G5 requirements  
**Previous authorities:** ROADMAP-002A, DR-STRATEGY-002, DR-CATALOGUE-002

## 1. Context

Catalogue Batch C-001 proved that the lean C0–C3 path can publish useful profiles quickly, but the first five profiles reached production with governed visual gaps because the existing media threshold required both strong reuse rights and strong specimen-identity evidence for an approved primary visual.

The Project Owner has determined that a broad public Atlas must normally show useful cultivar imagery and that Catalogue media should distinguish legal reuse permission from cultivar-identity confidence instead of treating both as one binary approval threshold.

## 2. Owner decision

The Project Owner approves a Visual-First Catalogue amendment under which Catalogue Profiles should normally display at least three lawful visual items:

1. a mature or whole-plant habit image;
2. a foliage-detail image;
3. a seasonal or diagnostic image.

Up to five images are encouraged where useful lawful material is available.

Catalogue visuals may use any of these identity-confidence states:

- `documented`;
- `source-asserted`;
- `community-identified`.

Every displayed item must have a lawful reuse basis, complete attribution and conspicuous item-level identity-confidence disclosure.

## 3. Binding legal and editorial boundary

- Attribution or a copyright warning does not create permission to copy, cache, transform or display an image.
- An image with unresolved reuse rights may remain an internal or outbound reference candidate but may not be served by the Atlas.
- Source-asserted and community-identified images are illustrative and must not be represented as independently authenticated cultivar evidence.
- Visual resemblance, filename, nursery label, garden sign or community assertion may support a qualified identity state but does not establish clonal authentication.
- Generic species photographs and photographs of a different cultivar remain prohibited as substitutes.

## 4. Public presentation requirements

For each displayed Catalogue image, the interface must disclose:

- creator and source;
- lawful reuse basis and licence or permission where applicable;
- visual role;
- identity-confidence state;
- identification basis;
- an explicit qualification when the item is not independently verified by the Atlas.

A source-asserted or community-identified image should include language equivalent to:

> Source-identified or community-identified; not independently authenticated by the Japanese Maple Atlas.

Catalogue profiles should invite users to submit photographs, corrections or corroborating identification information through a governed contribution path.

## 5. C2 amendment

For new Catalogue production, the normal C2 target becomes a minimum three-item lawful visual gallery covering habit, foliage and seasonal or diagnostic representation.

A profile may proceed with fewer than three only through an explicit recorded exception that identifies the missing role and preserves an active backfill task.

A governed visual gap remains available where lawful imagery cannot be obtained, but it is no longer the preferred routine outcome.

## 6. Batch application

- C-001 receives immediate visual backfill under this policy.
- C-002 and subsequent Catalogue batches use the Visual-First standard from the beginning.
- The adult or whole-plant habit image should normally be the card and profile-primary image.
- Additional gallery items should normally include foliage detail and seasonal or diagnostic context.
- Reference Standard G5 remains unchanged.

## 7. Authority and implementation

This decision authorises engineering to implement:

- a CUL-compatible Catalogue media contract;
- three-to-five-image galleries;
- card-primary selection;
- item-level rights and identity-confidence metadata;
- attribution and qualification rendering;
- correction, confirmation and photo-submission entry points;
- revised Catalogue C2 validation and controlled exceptions.

It authorises media to search broadly across lawful sources and to use source-asserted or community-identified Catalogue visuals when transparently qualified.

It authorises editorial to define visual roles, review captions and uncertainty language, and support community-confirmation wording without being required to authenticate every photographed clone conclusively.

## 8. Unchanged boundaries

- RC-001 through RC-005 remain frozen public Reference Standards.
- RC-006 through RC-010 remain G4 PASS, G5 BLOCKED and non-public.
- No Reference Standard may satisfy G5 solely through the relaxed Catalogue identity-confidence standard.
- No unresolved-rights image becomes lawful merely through attribution, disclaimer or external availability.
