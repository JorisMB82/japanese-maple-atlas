## 8. Completion checklist for the first G5 package

A record reaches G5 PASS only after all of the following are present:

- selected source asset and final visual role;
- item-level creator, contributor, rights holder and exact licence/permission;
- original bytes beneath `atlas-repository/media-sources/`;
- Atlas SHA-256 and technical metadata;
- completed `MEDIA-PROVENANCE_<MEDIA-ID>.md`;
- documented specimen-identity basis, confidence and editorial status;
- private EXIF/GPS review and public metadata stripping decision;
- image-specific caption, alt text and focal point;
- four processor-generated derivatives;
- visual review of orientation, crop, distortion, no-upscale result and identity context;
- approved media sidecar;
- successful repository-quality workflow.

## 9. Stop rule

If permission or original delivery remains unavailable, keep the candidate under review or seek an owner-approved governed gap. Do not convert an external delay into a PASS or dilute the legal and identity gates.
