## Botanical and editorial determination

Institutional sources support a dense, upright, compact shrub or small tree with small five- to seven-lobed green leaves that are crinkled or curled and held in close tufts. The “lion’s mane” effect arises from individual leaf morphology, close leaf placement and whole-plant density together.

Autumn descriptions range from soft orange to gold and crimson and remain condition-qualified. Descriptive confidence is high; authentication from one curled leaf, a common name, label or photograph remains low.

## Naming and identity decisions

- ‘Shishi-gashira’ remains the locked hyphenated working form.
- ‘Shishigashira’ remains an institutional/search form, not a separate cultivar.
- RHS-recorded ‘Ribesifolium’, ‘Crispifolium’ and ‘Mejishi’ remain synonyms with unresolved historical priority and scope.
- RHS prose typo “Shish geshira” is explicitly rejected.
