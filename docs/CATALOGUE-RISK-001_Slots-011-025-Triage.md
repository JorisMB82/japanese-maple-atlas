## 1. Method

Risk is assigned to the specific Catalogue production problem, not to the horticultural merit of the plant.

- **LOW:** accepted name and taxon are consistent; core descriptions are compatible; no material identity dispute is visible.
- **MEDIUM:** one bounded issue requires targeted review, such as romanisation, synonym scope, display-name ambiguity, dimensions or a limited source conflict.
- **HIGH:** identity, taxon, synonymy, commercial renaming, core morphology or source sufficiency is materially unstable. High-risk records do not enter an ordinary Catalogue batch.

A medium-risk classification does not require a monograph. It requires one clearly defined review question, an independent corroborating source where feasible and an explicit conclusion or retained uncertainty.

## 5. Commercial-source qualification

A nursery or breeder source may support:

- current trade spelling;
- current commercial alias usage;
- availability;
- a clearly attributed introduction claim;
- a media lead subject to separate rights review.

It may not by itself establish:

- accepted taxon;
- synonym equivalence;
- original-clone identity;
- universal mature dimensions;
- a disputed historical origin;
- a core diagnostic claim contradicted by institutional sources.

Where commercial material is the only source for a material claim, the claim remains qualified or omitted.

## 8. Stop rules

A proposed profile becomes **BLOCKED / DO NOT USE IN ROUTINE CATALOGUE** when any of the following occurs:

1. supported taxon cannot be established;
2. duplicate review finds a probable existing cultivar entity;
3. a commercial alias may represent more than one clone;
4. credible sources contradict the core identity or diagnostic morphology;
5. fewer than the permitted minimum credible sources support the useful profile;
6. the medium-risk issue cannot be bounded without Reference Standard-level research.

A blocked slot returns to the Project Owner for replacement or explicit Reference Standard nomination. It does not silently proceed as a low-confidence public profile.
