## Decision

The Project Owner approves the conservative editorial treatments recorded in `DR-EDITORIAL-002` for RC-006, RC-007 and RC-008.

This approval resolves only the bounded G4 nomenclatural and provenance conditions identified by the editorial review.

### RC-006 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Osakazuki’

Approved treatment:

- retain ‘Osakazuki’ as the Atlas working form;
- retain ‘Ōsakazuki’ as the RHS display form;
- retain ‘Taihai’ as an RHS-recorded synonym of unresolved historical scope;
- retain Amoenum, Elegans and Heptalobum formulations as source-specific horticultural-group formulations rather than separate identities or clonal proof;
- treat JC Raulston’s ‘Ōsakasuki’ as an unresolved record spelling, not an approved synonym;
- preserve the absence of primary provenance as an explicit research gap.

### RC-007 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Butterfly’

Approved treatment:

- retain ‘Butterfly’ as the Atlas working form;
- retain ‘Kochō-no-mai’ as an RHS-recorded synonym while its historical sequence and primary provenance remain unresolved;
- preserve ‘White Butterfly’ as a separate cultivar;
- do not convert unverified commercial aliases or chronology into established fact.

### RC-008 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Shishi-gashira’

Approved treatment:

- retain ‘Shishi-gashira’ as the Atlas working form;
- retain ‘Ribesifolium’, ‘Crispifolium’ and ‘Mejishi’ as recorded synonyms while historical priority and scope remain unresolved;
- retain ‘Shishigashira’ as a search or institutional variant;
- reject “Shish geshira” as a typographical form rather than a governed synonym.
