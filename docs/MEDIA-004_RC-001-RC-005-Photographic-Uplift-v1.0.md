## Operating rule

The existing identity plates remain approved release-minimum visuals. New photography augments them unless a governed replacement demonstrably improves rights, traceability, botanical usefulness or coverage. Visual attractiveness alone is not a replacement rationale.

Every candidate remains unapproved until item-level rights, original-byte preservation, specimen identity, privacy, metadata and derivative validation are complete.

## Cross-record capture standard

Habit views should show the complete crown and visible structural framework where practical. Record season, location granularity, accession/identity basis, approximate age or size class and any pruning, graft or training context that materially affects form.

Diagnostic-detail views should show sufficient resolution for leaf division, lobe shape, margins, petiole, bark or fruit. Use a scale where editorially useful. Avoid colour claims from heavily edited or undocumented files.

## RC-001 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Bloodgood’

Priority: mature broad-upright habit and non-dissected palmate foliage detail. Enhanced targets are spring emergence, summer dark red-purple foliage, autumn red/crimson colour, flowers and samaras.

Do not imply that red-purple foliage alone authenticates the cultivar or that one crown shape and colour are universal.

## RC-002 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Seiryu’

Priority: upright or vase-shaped whole-plant habit plus deeply dissected green foliage, preferably from the same documented accession. Enhanced targets are spring red tips/margins and the autumn colour sequence.

Do not imply that dissected foliage requires a cascading habit.

## RC-003 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Sango-kaku’

Priority: winter coral-pink/red young twigs or branches, then upright/vase habit and seasonal foliage. Distinguish young wood from mature trunk bark and document season.

Do not imply that mature bark must retain the same coral intensity as young winter growth.

## RC-004 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Crimson Queen’

Priority: arching, pendulous or cascading habit plus deeply dissected foliage. Record graft height, light and season where relevant. Enhanced targets are spring, summer and autumn pigment changes.

Do not imply that every branch hangs vertically or that foliage remains one colour throughout the year.

## RC-005 — *Acer shirasawanum* ‘Aureum’

Priority: spring and summer foliage under documented exposure, then upright-rounded/broad-rounded habit. Enhanced targets are rounded shallowly lobed leaves, flowers and red upright samaras.

Do not imply one permanent gold colour or apply generic *Acer palmatum* traits automatically to this *Acer shirasawanum* cultivar.

## Acceptance criteria

An augmentation asset must have explicit rights, preserved source bytes and SHA-256, recorded specimen identity/confidence, matching subject/season, completed caption/alt/focal point, documented EXIF/GPS treatment and passing JPEG/PNG derivative validation. It must improve a recorded coverage gap without overstating authentication.
