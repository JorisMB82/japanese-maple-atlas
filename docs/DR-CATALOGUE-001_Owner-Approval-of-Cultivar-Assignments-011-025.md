## 6. Promotion boundary

Approval as a Catalogue assignment does not imply future Reference Standard status. Promotion requires a separately governed nomination, decision and Reference Standard workflow. No `RC-###` identifier is allocated by this decision.

A later assignment change requires:

- a documented owner amendment;
- duplicate and alias impact review;
- registry version increment;
- reconciliation of any in-progress editorial or media work;
- confirmation that no obsolete assignment remains discoverable or publishable.
