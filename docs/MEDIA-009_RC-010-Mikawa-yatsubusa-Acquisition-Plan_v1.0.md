## Candidate route B — David J. Stang / Wikimedia Commons

`Acer palmatum Yatsubusa 0zz.jpg` is a 1,000 × 665 JPEG photographed at the United States National Arboretum on 19 April 2006 by David J. Stang and licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Wikimedia categorises it under *Acer palmatum* ‘Mikawa yatsubusa’, while the file description uses the shorter label ‘Yatsubusa’.

This naming mismatch is material. The asset remains a candidate until the depicted plant's label/accession can be confirmed as ‘Mikawa-yatsubusa’. The image may provide spring whole-plant or layered-foliage context, but must not be approved merely from category placement or appearance.

Required before selection: original bytes, permanent file-page revision, exact CC attribution/change notice, Atlas SHA-256, visual review, identity clarification and public EXIF/GPS stripping.

Disposition: `identity-review`.
