## 4. Evidence and locator summary

### Identity and naming

- Accepted Atlas form: ‘Shishi-gashira’.
- Institutional/search variant: ‘Shishigashira’.
- RHS synonyms: ‘Ribesifolium’, ‘Crispifolium’, ‘Mejishi’.
- Missouri independently records ‘Ribesifolium’ synonymy.
- RHS prose typo “Shish geshira” is explicitly rejected.
- “Lion’s head” is retained as a source-reported interpretation, not an origin history.

### Morphology and architecture

- Dense, bushy, upright and compact habit.
- Small five- to seven-lobed green leaves.
- Crinkled/curled texture.
- Leaves held in close tufts along stems.
- Young plants may appear sparse.
- “Tufted” is not converted into unsupported botanical whorling.

### Seasonal expression

- Early green spring foliage with reported early leaf-out.
- Dense green summer texture.
- Autumn range: gold and soft orange through crimson.
- Small reddish-purple flowers and later samaras are secondary characters.

### Dimensions

- RHS: 2.5–4 m × 1.5–2.5 m, 20–50 years.
- Missouri: 7–15 ft × 5–12 ft, slow growing.
- JC Raulston: 15-ft summary size; accession xx0090 measured 8 ft on 9 November 2023.

No universal maximum, dwarf classification or annual increment is approved.

### Cultivation

- Full sun to partial shade, with hot-climate afternoon shade qualification.
- Moist, organically rich, well-drained, acid to neutral/slightly acidic soil.
- High-pH chlorosis risk.
- Hot-sun/moisture-deficit scorch risk.
- Early leaf-out and late-frost risk.
- RHS H6 and USDA zones 5–8 remain separate frameworks.

### History

- RHS herbarium: 24 September 1982, 14 April 2000 and 18 October 2000.
- JC Raulston: living accession and forty photographs spanning multiple seasons.
- Original selector, date, location, earliest catalogue and synonym priority remain unresolved.
