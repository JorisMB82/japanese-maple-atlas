# CATALOGUE-VISUAL-ROLES-003 — C-003 Editorial Guidance

**Status:** EDITORIAL GUIDANCE — media selection and rights review remain pending  
**Applies to:** CUL-000021 through CUL-000025  
**Authority:** DR-STRATEGY-003, ROADMAP-002B and CATALOGUE-002  
**Editorial boundary:** Defines useful subjects, supported caption facts and identity qualifications; does not approve rights, assets, media sidecars, C2 or publication.

## 1. Shared identity-confidence and contribution wording

A lawful `source-asserted` or `community-identified` image may illustrate a Catalogue Profile when its identification basis is disclosed, the image does not contradict the profile and it is not used as sole evidence for accepted name, taxon, synonymy or a diagnostic conclusion.

Use this qualification for a non-documented item:

> The source or contributor identifies this plant as the named cultivar. The Japanese Maple Atlas has not independently authenticated the clone. Corrections and corroborating photographs are welcome.

Use this contribution invitation, adapted to the cultivar and missing role:

> Have a clearer photograph or evidence for this identification? Submit the original image, date, general location, plant label or provenance, and any accession or purchase record through the Atlas contribution path.

## 2. CUL-000021 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Tamukeyama’

- **Habit-primary:** a mature broad mound showing arching, weeping and cascading branches, with enough scale to distinguish a whole plant from a trained standard.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached finely dissected leaves showing the purple-to-burgundy spring or greenish-purple summer colour and the feathery divisions.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** an autumn branch or whole-plant view showing the orange-to-bright-red transition.
- **Optional roles:** spring flush; side profile showing spread relative to height.
- **Visual confusion risks:** RC-004 ‘Crimson Queen’, CUL-000013 ‘Inaba-shidare’, CUL-000020 ‘Red Dragon’, CUL-000022 ‘Orangeola’ and generic red dissectum material.
- **Caption facts supported:** mound-forming cascading habit; finely dissected foliage; purple or burgundy spring leaves; greenish-purple summer; orange and brighter-red autumn; source-dependent dimensions. Support: `SRC-CUL-000021-01` and `SRC-CUL-000021-02`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** that one red laceleaf photograph conclusively separates the cultivar; universal summer colour retention; a fixed maximum size; the historical scope of ‘Beni-hagoromo’.
- **Identity disclosure:** use the shared non-documented qualification unless a meaningful accession or propagation trail supports `documented`.
- **Contribution prompt:** request a whole-plant side view, attached leaf close-up, label or accession record, and an autumn view from the same plant.

## 3. CUL-000022 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Orangeola’

- **Habit-primary:** a mature mound-forming plant with arching branches, photographed without pruning or staking that conceals the natural outline.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached dissected leaves showing orange-red emergence or reddish-green summer colour, with date and exposure recorded.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** the brighter orange-red autumn display, preferably at branch and whole-plant scale.
- **Optional roles:** repeat spring flush later in the season; scale-context image showing the relatively compact crown.
- **Visual confusion risks:** ‘Tamukeyama’, ‘Red Dragon’, ‘Inaba-shidare’, ‘Crimson Queen’ and unnamed red-orange laceleaf maples.
- **Caption facts supported:** mound-forming and arching habit; orange-red spring foliage; reddish-green summer foliage; brighter orange-red autumn; dissectum foliage. Support: `SRC-CUL-000022-01` and `SRC-CUL-000022-02`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** one fixed orange shade; colour persistence across climates; claims that orange foliage alone authenticates the clone; exact mature dimensions.
- **Identity disclosure:** source-identified or community-identified photographs must state that the Atlas has not independently authenticated the clone.
- **Contribution prompt:** request dated photographs of spring, midsummer and autumn from the same labelled plant, plus whole-plant habit and provenance.

## 4. CUL-000023 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Higasa-yama’

- **Habit-primary:** a mature upright, broad-crowned specimen showing scale and branching rather than only the spectacular spring flush.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached spring leaves while tightly unfurling and after expansion, showing crinkled seven-lobed form and cream, pink and green variegation.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** paired spring and summer views from the same plant demonstrating how the strong variegation becomes greener or changes through the season.
- **Optional roles:** autumn orange-yellow-red foliage; branch-level view of weak or partly green variegation.
- **Visual confusion risks:** RC-007 ‘Butterfly’, other variegated Japanese maples, `Higasayama` spelling variants, and near-names such as ‘Shin-higasa’. Generic pink spring foliage is not sufficient.
- **Caption facts supported:** accepted hyphenated identity; spaced and joined spelling variants; seven slender crinkled lobes; spring cream-pink-green variegation; greener summer foliage; upright medium-to-large garden framework. Support: `SRC-CUL-000023-01` through `SRC-CUL-000023-04`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** stable variegation on every leaf or in every year; any claim that green shoots are automatically reversion; unresolved historical synonyms; universal mature size.
- **Identity disclosure:** make the asserted basis conspicuous because seasonal pattern volatility can make visual comparison unreliable.
- **Contribution prompt:** request a same-plant seasonal sequence, label or accession evidence, and photographs showing both strongly and weakly variegated growth.

## 5. CUL-000024 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Arakawa’

- **Habit-primary:** an upright mature tree showing crown form and trunk, with a scale reference that does not obscure the bark.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached five-lobed green leaves with twig and branch context; foliage is supporting, not the principal identity claim.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** close and medium views of rough corky bark on older trunk or branches, plus enough plant context to show that the image is not generic bark texture.
- **Optional roles:** smooth young growth contrasted with older corked wood; golden-yellow to orange autumn foliage.
- **Visual confusion risks:** seedlings or other rough-bark maples, ‘Arakawa-ukon’, material labelled `ex Arakawa`, RC-003 ‘Sango-kaku’, ‘Beni-kawa’ and ‘Aoyagi’.
- **Caption facts supported:** vigorous upright habit; rough corky bark develops after several years and strengthens with age; small green five-lobed leaves; golden-yellow and orange autumn colour. Support: `SRC-CUL-000024-01` and `SRC-CUL-000024-02`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** exact age at first corking; bark texture as sole authentication; the English labels ‘Rough Bark’ or ‘Rough Bark Maple’ as exclusive cultivar names; fixed mature size.
- **Identity disclosure:** source-asserted bark photographs must say that the photographed clone has not been independently authenticated.
- **Contribution prompt:** request labelled whole-plant, trunk and branch images with planting age or provenance, and a clear distinction between young smooth and older corked wood.

## 6. CUL-000025 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Red Pygmy’

- **Habit-primary:** an unobstructed whole plant showing the bushy or mound-forming outline and actual scale; include age where known because credible size records diverge.
- **Foliage-detail:** attached leaves showing mostly seven long, narrow, nearly untoothed linearilobum lobes and red-to-brownish-red colour.
- **Seasonal-diagnostic:** spring and summer comparison showing red-purple emergence and any greener or bronzier summer shift; autumn red is a useful alternative.
- **Optional roles:** scale-context beside a measured support; side-by-side comparison with a green linearilobum cultivar where identities are independently documented.
- **Visual confusion risks:** CUL-000012 ‘Koto-no-ito’, ‘Villa Taranto’, ‘Sharp’s Pygmy’, ‘Coonara Pygmy’, other red narrow-lobed cultivars and cross-genus plants called ‘Red Pygmy’.
- **Caption facts supported:** accepted name; linearilobum group; mostly seven long narrow nearly entire lobes; red-purple to brownish-red foliage; slow mound-like habit; substantial source conflict in mature size. Support: `SRC-CUL-000025-01` through `SRC-CUL-000025-03`.
- **Claims requiring qualification:** guaranteed dwarf size; one exact autumn palette; leaf shape as sole clonal authentication; explanations for the 17-ft JC Raulston accession.
- **Identity disclosure:** use the shared qualification for any image lacking a documented accession or propagation basis.
- **Contribution prompt:** request a measured whole-plant image, leaf close-up, provenance, age and seasonal sequence, especially for mature specimens exceeding published compact ranges.

## 7. Media handoff boundary

Media remains responsible for lawful reuse basis, creator and rights-holder records, source bytes, privacy treatment, checksums, derivative generation, sidecars and C2 disposition. These editorial role notes may guide candidate selection and captions but do not approve any item.
