## 3. Assessment meaning

- **PASS:** editorial support is sufficient and the required gallery is already integrated, or the published profile and gallery are already live. A PASS for a non-public profile does not itself claim C3 publication.
- **CONDITIONAL:** editorial content and sources are sufficient, but media completion, rights/identity review, sidecar integration or another downstream C2/C3 condition remains.
- **BLOCKED:** a material editorial identity, naming, taxon or evidence defect prevents routine progression.

No cultivar is editorially BLOCKED.

## 6. Residual editorial controls

- Red dissectum captions must not convert comparative tendencies into authentication claims.
- Bark colour and roughness must be shown with plant age and whole-plant context where possible.
- Variegation must be dated or season-qualified and should not be represented as fixed across every leaf.
- Golden and orange foliage captions must distinguish taxon and seasonal context rather than relying on colour alone.
- Naming variants belong in identity and search handling; they must not generate separate stable identities.
- Size ranges remain source- and age-qualified, especially for ‘Red Pygmy’, ‘Aoyagi’, ‘Trompenburg’ and ‘Autumn Moon’.
