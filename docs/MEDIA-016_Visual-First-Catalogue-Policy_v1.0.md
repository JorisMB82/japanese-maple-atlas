# MEDIA-016 — Visual-First Catalogue Policy v1.0

**Status:** APPROVED AND ACTIVE  
**Authority:** DR-STRATEGY-003 and ROADMAP-002B  
**Applies to:** Catalogue media discovery, acquisition, processing and publication  
**Reference Standard effect:** none

## 1. Operating principle

Catalogue media should be visually useful, lawfully reusable and transparent about identity confidence.

Media reviewers must assess two independent questions:

1. **May the Atlas lawfully display and transform this item?**
2. **How strong is the evidence that the photographed subject is the named cultivar?**

A weak identity basis may be published with conspicuous qualification. An unresolved legal reuse basis may not.

## 2. Normal acquisition target

For each Catalogue Profile, seek three to five lawful items covering:

- mature or whole-plant habit;
- foliage detail;
- seasonal or diagnostic representation;
- optional alternate seasons or diagnostic details.

The habit image is normally the primary card and hero image.

## 3. Source discovery

Media may search broadly across:

- Wikimedia Commons and other item-level open repositories;
- public-domain institutional collections;
- Creative Commons collections;
- botanical gardens and arboreta offering permission;
- universities and public institutions;
- specialist nurseries and photographers offering permission;
- contributor-owned photographs;
- community submissions;
- archival collections where lawful reuse is documented.

Search breadth does not reduce the requirement for item-level rights evidence.

## 4. Rights disposition

### Displayable

An item may proceed when it has a documented lawful basis such as public domain, exact Creative Commons licence, explicit permission, contributor ownership or another approved open licence.

### Candidate only

An item with unresolved rights may be recorded with its source URL, creator where known, outreach status and intended role. It may not be copied, cached, processed into derivatives or displayed publicly.

Attribution, a source link, a copyright disclaimer, hotlinking or public availability do not convert a candidate into a displayable item.

## 5. Identity disposition

### Documented

Use when meaningful specimen, accession, determination or propagation evidence exists.

### Source-asserted

Use when the originating source identifies the cultivar but the Atlas has not independently authenticated the clone. Record the exact assertion basis, such as a garden sign, nursery label, institutional caption or photographer metadata.

### Community-identified

Use when a grower, photographer or contributor identifies the cultivar without independent confirmation. Record who made the assertion and invite corroboration.

Every non-documented item must carry a visible qualification and must not be treated as authentication evidence.

## 6. Item package

For each displayable item, preserve or record:

- stable `MED-CUL-######-###` ID;
- source locator and source item ID;
- creator and rights holder where known;
- lawful rights basis and exact licence or permission;
- attribution text;
- original source bytes where permitted;
- source checksum;
- privacy review;
- visual role and primary designation;
- identity-confidence state and basis;
- identity limitation;
- caption and alt text;
- deterministic derivatives;
- reviewer and revision history.

## 7. Selection quality

The primary image should show a useful adult or whole-plant habit wherever possible. A close leaf image should not be used as the default card image unless an explicit exception records that no lawful habit image is available.

Avoid near-duplicate images. A three-item package should communicate different roles rather than three similar photographs.

## 8. Community and correction loop

Media packages should support public actions to:

- submit a photograph;
- confirm an identification with evidence;
- report a possible error;
- offer permission or higher-resolution originals.

Incoming material remains a contribution candidate until rights, identity, privacy and technical review pass.

## 9. C-001 backfill

Reassess every C-001 candidate under the new identity-confidence model.

- Candidates previously rejected only because identity was sign-based, source-asserted or community-asserted may now qualify for Catalogue display if their reuse rights and technical requirements are complete.
- Candidates blocked by rights remain blocked.
- Aim for three lawful items for each of CUL-000011 through CUL-000015.
- Replace governed gaps incrementally when each profile has an adequate lawful package.

## 10. C-002 and later

Begin media discovery in parallel with editorial C0/C1 work. Return:

- intended three-to-five-item gallery;
- role coverage;
- primary-card recommendation;
- rights basis per item;
- identity-confidence state per item;
- exceptions and outstanding permission work;
- exact source and derivative records.

## 11. Reference Standard boundary

This policy does not relax Reference Standard G5. Catalogue source-asserted or community-identified images do not become Reference Standard evidence or satisfy G5 without separate approval under the Reference Standard media contract.
