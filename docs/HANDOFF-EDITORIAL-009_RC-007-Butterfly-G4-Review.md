## Botanical and editorial determination

Institutional sources support a rather upright, bushy shrub or small tree with five- to seven-lobed green or grey-green leaves carrying irregular cream-white margins, often pink-tinged. Pattern width and pink intensity vary. No precise cultivar-specific autumn trajectory or reversion frequency is approved.

Descriptive confidence is high for the combined habit and variegation profile. Appearance alone, one green shoot, one photograph or one label does not authenticate the clone.
