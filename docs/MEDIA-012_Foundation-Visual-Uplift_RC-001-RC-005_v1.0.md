## 1. Objective

Improve the public visual usefulness of the five frozen Reference Standards without replacing valid governed identity plates prematurely.

Each record receives one bounded uplift target. The existing approved illustration remains the release-minimum visual until a stronger governed photograph is fully approved.

## 2. Operating rules

- Do not delete, deprecate or replace an existing approved plate merely because a candidate photograph looks stronger.
- Treat the uplift as an additional governed asset unless a separate review explicitly approves replacement.
- Prefer a photograph that adds plant-scale or diagnostic information not already conveyed by the plate.
- Require the same rights, source-preservation, privacy, identity and derivative lineage controls as any other photograph.
- A nursery, garden or filename assertion remains an asserted identity unless strengthened by accession or determination evidence.
- Colour alone is never clonal authentication.

## 4. Candidate evaluation minimum

For each uplift candidate, media records:

- exact item URL and access date;
- creator, contributor and rights holder;
- displayed licence or permission requirement;
- whether the original file can be obtained;
- specimen identity basis and confidence;
- subject, season and date;
- likely public location granularity;
- technical resolution and orientation;
- whether the candidate adds genuinely new information;
- whether it risks displacing a valid plate without sufficient benefit.

## 5. Uplift completion rule

An uplift is complete only when:

1. a source original is preserved privately;
2. rights and attribution are approved;
3. specimen identity is transparently qualified;
4. caption and alt text are image-specific;
5. four derivatives are generated and visually inspected;
6. the relevant approved media sidecar is updated through the governed process;
7. repository validation passes.

Until then, the current identity plate remains the valid public visual and the uplift remains a candidate or explicit gap.

## 6. Sequencing and effort

Foundation uplift is intentionally secondary to completing the first Wave 1 G5 package.

Recommended sequence after RC-010/RC-009 permission requests are active:

1. RC-003 — highest informational gain through winter stems;
2. RC-002 — strong architectural clarification;
3. RC-004 — habit-led usefulness;
4. RC-005 — seasonal habit context;
5. RC-001 — mature habit context.

Expected media working effort per completed uplift is 4–8 hours after a suitable candidate and rights path are identified, excluding permission latency.
