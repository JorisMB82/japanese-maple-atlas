## Botanical and editorial determination

Institutional evidence supports a dense compact upright or young vase-shaped plant becoming more rounded with age. Its governing diagnostic is the seasonal trajectory: yellow-orange to chartreuse spring emergence with orange or pink margins, summer green and orange/yellow autumn colour.

RHS reports five to seven lobes and Missouri Botanical Garden five; this source difference is preserved. Timing and colour do not independently authenticate the clone.

## Naming and identity decisions

- ‘Katsura’ remains the locked cultivar epithet.
- No cultivar synonym is approved from the reviewed institutional set.
- The record is explicitly separated from the unrelated katsura tree, *Cercidiphyllum japonicum*.
- No literal translation, naming rationale or geographic origin is approved without primary evidence.
