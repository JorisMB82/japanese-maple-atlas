# HANDOFF-EDITORIAL-CATALOGUE-003 — Visual Roles and C-002 Support

**Status:** ACTIVE — READY FOR EDITORIAL EXECUTION AFTER GOVERNANCE MERGE  
**Authority:** DR-STRATEGY-003, ROADMAP-002B and CATALOGUE-002  
**Recommended branch:** `content/catalogue-c-002`

## 1. Objective

Support Visual-First Catalogue production without turning editorial into a full photographic-authentication programme.

Editorial defines what imagery is useful, ensures captions and uncertainty statements match the botanical profile, and completes C-002 C0/C1 work. Media remains responsible for rights, provenance, source assets and technical image packages.

## 2. C-001 visual backfill support

For CUL-000011 through CUL-000015, provide a concise visual-role note identifying:

- the preferred adult or whole-plant habit view;
- the most useful foliage-detail view;
- the most useful seasonal or diagnostic view;
- important comparisons or misidentification risks;
- wording that explains source-asserted or community-identified status without undermining the profile;
- a short invitation for users to submit a better image or corroborating evidence.

Review media captions and identity qualifications when media returns candidate packages. Do not approve reuse rights or source-byte processing.

## 3. C-002 C0/C1 production

Produce canonical, non-public Catalogue Profile inputs for:

- CUL-000016 — *Acer shirasawanum* ‘Autumn Moon’;
- CUL-000017 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Waterfall’;
- CUL-000018 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Aoyagi’;
- CUL-000019 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Shin-deshōjō’;
- CUL-000020 — *Acer palmatum* ‘Red Dragon’.

Complete:

- routine duplicate and naming review for the LOW-risk records;
- targeted taxon and alias review for ‘Autumn Moon’;
- targeted romanisation and label-variant review for ‘Shin-deshōjō’;
- lean source records;
- risk confirmation;
- useful profile content;
- preferred visual roles for each cultivar.

## 4. Visual-role fields returned to media

For each C-002 profile, identify:

- `habit-primary` subject recommendation;
- `foliage-detail` subject recommendation;
- `seasonal-diagnostic` subject recommendation;
- optional fourth and fifth roles;
- cultivar comparisons that imagery could easily confuse;
- caption facts supported by the editorial sources;
- statements that must remain qualified;
- public correction and contribution invitation.

## 5. Identity-confidence wording

Editorial may accept lawful source-asserted or community-identified imagery for Catalogue illustration when the item is clearly labelled and does not contradict the profile.

Recommended qualification:

> The source or contributor identifies this plant as the named cultivar. The Japanese Maple Atlas has not independently authenticated the clone. Corrections and corroborating photographs are welcome.

Editorial must not use a photograph as sole evidence for accepted name, taxon, synonymy or diagnostic certainty.

## 6. Deliverables

Return a protected editorial PR with:

- five C-002 C0-complete, C1-ready canonical inputs;
- visual-role notes for C-001 and C-002;
- source counts, targeted-review outcomes and residual issues;
- exact caption/qualification guidance;
- no media asset approval, schema change, application change or publication claim.
