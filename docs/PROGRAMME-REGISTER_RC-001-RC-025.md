## 2. Operating rule

This register records repository-authoritative state. Assignment approval authorises production but does not equal publication. Catalogue publication requires C0–C3; Reference Standard publication requires G0–G9.

The two workflows are:

- **Reference Standard:** G0–G9 with individual Project Owner G8 freeze and unchanged G5 requirements.
- **Catalogue Profile:** C0–C3 with owner-approved assignments, Visual-First lawful media and protected integration.

Catalogue rights and specimen-identity confidence are governed separately. A source-asserted or community-identified image may be displayed with conspicuous qualification when reuse is lawful. A rights-unresolved image may not be displayed.

## 5. Visual-First Catalogue contract

A normal Catalogue gallery targets three to five lawful items:

1. `habit-primary`;
2. `foliage-detail`;
3. `seasonal-diagnostic`;
4. optional complementary views up to five total.

Every displayed item requires a lawful reuse basis, source and attribution, item-level identity-confidence state, identification basis and qualification, and privacy and technical validation.

Controlled identity-confidence states are `documented`, `source-asserted` and `community-identified`. Rights-unresolved material remains candidate-only. A fewer-than-three role exception or governed visual gap must be explicit, temporary and measurable.

Current public cultivar total after this tranche: **13** — five Reference Standards plus eight Catalogue Profiles.

## 8. Gate definitions

### Catalogue

- **C0 — Assigned and duplicate-checked:** owner-approved assignment, stable identity, supported taxon, duplicate/alias review and initial risk.
- **C1 — Content and evidence ready:** lean profile, compact sources, risk qualification and editorial approval.
- **C2 — Visual-First media resolved:** normally a validated lawful gallery covering habit, foliage and seasonal or diagnostic roles; controlled exception or governed gap where necessary.
- **C3 — Validated and batch-published:** schema/semantic validation, protected CI, production inspection, release evidence and rollback readiness.

### Reference Standard

- **G0–G4:** assignment, research, evidence, draft and botanical/editorial approval.
- **G5:** approved primary visual or owner-approved governed gap with complete Reference Standard media governance.
- **G6–G7:** integration and technical validation.
- **G8:** Project Owner freeze and publication.
- **G9:** post-publication inspection.
