## Botanical and editorial determination

Institutional sources support the working name ‘Osakazuki’, RHS display form ‘Ōsakazuki’, rounded-to-upright architecture, relatively large usually seven-lobed green foliage and brilliant orange-scarlet to crimson/red autumn expression. Dimensions and hardiness remain source-specific. Scorch resistance is not stated absolutely.

Descriptive confidence is high for the combined profile. Clonal authentication from morphology, autumn colour, a label or one accession record remains low.

## Naming and identity decisions

- ‘Osakazuki’ remains the locked Atlas working spelling.
- ‘Ōsakazuki’ remains the RHS display/romanization form.
- ‘Taihai’ remains an RHS-recorded synonym with unresolved historical scope.
- Amoenum, Elegans and Heptalobum remain source-specific horticultural-group formulations, not interchangeable identity proofs.
- JC Raulston ‘Ōsakasuki’ remains an unresolved record spelling, not an approved synonym.

## Material unresolved issues

## Rejected claims retained

Autumn colour, seven lobes or basal cupping alone do not identify the cultivar; group labels are not automatically equivalent; full sun does not eliminate scorch risk; labels do not authenticate all commercial material; seedlings do not inherit cultivar identity.
