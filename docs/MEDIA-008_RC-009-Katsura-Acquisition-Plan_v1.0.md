## Required coverage

1. whole-plant early-spring habit;
2. bud-break and first-leaf sequence;
3. scaled leaf detail showing blade and margin colour;
4. same-accession transition into green summer foliage;
5. autumn orange/yellow expression;
6. age-context or crown-development view;
7. metadata explicitly preventing confusion with *Cercidiphyllum japonicum*.

Summer-only coverage is inadequate. Frost-damaged foliage must not represent normal expression. One yellow-orange early leaf is not cultivar-identity evidence, and seasonal marginal colour is not structural variegation equivalent to RC-007.
